Support

Our support team is here to help you with any questions, technical issues, or concerns related to the HM Hospital Management System. We strive to provide timely and effective assistance to all users.

Contact Information

  • Email Support: support@hmsystem.com
  • Phone: +1 (555) 123-4567
  • Business Hours: Monday – Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST
  • Emergency: For medical emergencies, please contact your local emergency services or visit the nearest emergency room.

Response Times

  • General Inquiries: Within 24 business hours
  • Technical Issues: Within 4–8 business hours
  • Urgent Account or Access Problems: Within 1–2 business hours
  • Billing and Invoice Disputes: Within 3 business days

Common Issues and Quick Fixes

  • Login Problems: Ensure you are using the correct username and password. Use the "Forgot Password" link to reset your credentials if needed. If your account is locked, contact support for assistance.
  • Appointment Scheduling Issues: Verify that the selected time slot is still available. If a booking fails, try refreshing the page or selecting an alternative doctor or time.
  • Missing Medical Records or Lab Reports: Records may take time to be uploaded by your doctor. If a report is marked as "Ready" but you cannot access it, try clearing your browser cache or using a different browser.
  • Prescription or Reminder Notifications: Check your notification settings in the "Settings" page to ensure reminders and alerts are enabled. Verify that your device allows notifications from our domain.
  • Technical Difficulties: Clear your browser cache and cookies, ensure your browser is up to date, or try accessing the platform from a different device or network.

Submit a Support Ticket

For issues that require detailed attention, please email support@hmsystem.com with the following information:

  • Your full name and username
  • A clear description of the issue or request
  • Steps to reproduce the problem (if applicable)
  • Screenshots or error messages
  • Your preferred contact method and time availability

Feedback and Feature Requests

We value your feedback. If you have suggestions for improving the platform or ideas for new features, please share them with us at feedback@hmsystem.com. Your input helps us build a better healthcare experience for everyone.
